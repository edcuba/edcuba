Eduard Cuba is a Machine Learning and AI Engineer based in Zürich, Switzerland, working as a Research Engineer at Oracle, where he leads R&D projects in data integration and information extraction using large language models, agentic AI, and graph machine learning.

He previously worked on AI for sustainability research at the University of Zürich, on machine learning for high-energy physics at CERN, and spent several years as a full-stack and backend engineer building distributed systems — including code running on millions of Linux systems worldwide as part of Red Hat's package management stack.

Experience

Eduard is currently a Senior Member of Technical Staff at Oracle, focusing on LLM-driven data integration and agentic AI. His earlier roles span research at CERN and the University of Zürich, and engineering positions at Dreamlab Technologies, Procivis, and Red Hat.

Main article: Experience

Projects

Alongside his core roles, Eduard has contributed to Wayflow, Oracle's declarative framework for enterprise AI agents; advised an MRV startup on AI for biodiversity monitoring; built an AI-powered FAQ generation tool; and co-founded a public transport optimization startup.

Main article: Projects

Research

His published work includes a master's thesis on particle shower reconstruction using graph neural networks at CERN, a paper on visual analysis of time-stamped event sequences (IEEE TVCG), and research on benchmarking AI workloads on HPC infrastructure.

Main article: Research

Open Source

Eduard is a contributor to dnf and libdnf, the package management tools used across RPM-based Linux distributions, and has made minor contributions to several security and ML tools.
